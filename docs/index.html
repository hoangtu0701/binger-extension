What you get

Everything you need for movie night

A Chrome extension that turns phimbro.com into a synchronized cinema for up to three people.

Playback in lockstep

Play, pause, and seek for everyone at once. If one person starts buffering, the room waits. Nobody watches a scene alone.

Live chat

Typing indicators, grouped messages, hover an avatar to see who sent what, and message bubbles that take on whatever theme the room is wearing.

Video calls

Peer to peer video and audio for the whole room, with STUN and TURN fallback for awkward networks. Speaker and headphone modes for clean movie audio.

Rooms you can lock

Six digit codes, up to three people, and a lock the host can flip at any time. Locked rooms ask newcomers for a four digit password.

Soundboard

Five synced sound effects, ten floating reactions, and pin mode to stick an emoji on the exact spot of the frame you are yelling about.

Fullscreen mode

Video call, chat, and soundboard snap into one connected panel beneath the film. Toggling fullscreen no longer drops the call for anyone.

Get started

Installed in under a minute

Pick a username, pick a password, and you are in. Everyone at the movie night needs Binger installed.

Hit the download button above to grab Binger.zip

Right click the zip, choose Extract All, and open the folder

Open chrome://extensions and switch on Developer mode

Click Load unpacked and pick the folder you just extracted

That is it. Open phimbro.com, pick a username, and make a room.
